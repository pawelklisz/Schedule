Project

One ISO date per line. These are skipped along with the days off when the schedule counts working days.

The days the schedule counts as working days. Tick all seven for work that goes on at weekends: a task then takes its days straight through. Changing it moves dates.

WBS numbers renumber themselves as tasks move. The key stays underneath, for links and history; search finds either, and a task's drawer shows both.

New tasks are numbered with this, from 1: HOME-1, HOME-2. Tasks you have keep their numbers, which links, history and the other device know them by. Blank carries on as they are.

Columns

Order is the board, left to right. Tick Started on the columns where work is under way: a card moved into one gets today as its start date and an amber bar, and in any other column before Done it has not started. Stale after flags a card that has sat in the column that many calendar days — it is a badge, nothing moves on its own. Leave blank for no limit.

A task with sub-items is a group, and never a card anyway. This also keeps tasks at those levels off the board while nothing is under them yet.

days

A column ticked Done shows the cards finished in that many days; older ones fold into a line at its foot. Blank shows them all.

Fields

Custom fields appear on every task and as columns in the CSV export.

People

A point is about an hour of work. For each day, the top box is how many points of daytime work someone has room for and the bottom box how many in the evening; the Load view plans work into them. Week is the total: type a new one and the days scale to it, or leave it blank for no limit — their work still shows, it is just never flagged. Tasks without points are not counted at all. Renaming someone here renames them on their tasks too.

Sprints

Make sprints by themselves on the same days each week, a weekend say. They are made a few weeks ahead and named by date. A week whose days are all holidays gets none, and one with some holidays a shorter one. Each can still be changed, or deleted, on its own in the Sprints view; one deleted stays deleted.

made weeks ahead

Key dates

Dates the project answers to: a deadline from outside, a delivery, a booking. In a task's drawer it can start after one (moving the key date moves it) or be due by one (a deadline: it never moves the task, but missing it shows as negative float). Each is a line across the chart.
